Data Integrity Standards

Zero-Fabrication Policy: During periods when the ESP32 node is disconnected or offline, environmental sensor columns (MQ-135, Temperature, Humidity, PIR, Fan) are recorded as empty fields in data/log.csv. They are never substituted with artificial zeros. This preserves research integrity when computing retrospective statistics or training models.
